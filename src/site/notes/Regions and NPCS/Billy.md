The new, ever-so-shy companion of Maristela and Penn, Billy was a druid who up until recently had been assisting [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Savras Lyrell\|Savras Lyrell]] and [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]] in the brewing of various healing potions and healing salves. Keen to learn more about the world and its flora, Savras recommended that he perhaps travel with the party to see more. 

Perhaps not quite realising what he signed up for, Billy readily accepted.
