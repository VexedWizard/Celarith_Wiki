An entity (known as "djinn") with peculiar mannerisms and perspectives, Gazre-Azram is a spirit from another place (and perhaps time) who was summoned to Celarith and trapped in a book by [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Zikran the Particularly Dense\|Zikran the Particularly Dense]].  Zikran, in his research on the other planes of existence, sought Gazre-Azram's aid in providing knowledge of such places and what one could find there. Although Gazre-Azram certainly has good perspective, Zikran's deal with him stated that he would let the djinn go once Gazre-Azram had detailed all he knew about the universe. Zikran did not stick to this bargain, and as such Gazre-Azram refused to assist Zikran any further. 


Once Zikran understood that this creature was not going to be of any further assistance, he left the entity in the book in his island study. Gazre-Azram reports hearing certain experiments occur in the lab, but was unsure of what occurred and when. What he does know for certain, though, was that at some point a very dark and eldritch presence made itself manifest in the cave, and didn't leave.
