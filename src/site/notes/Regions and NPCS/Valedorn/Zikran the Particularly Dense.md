Of all the mages working to understand the worlds beyond, Zikran's theories are by far the most ridiculed. Maristela recalls that she only truly knows of his name because of derogatory jokes at Zikran's expense (for it was his colleagues who gave him the epitaph 'The Particularly Dense'). Zikran's books are never reprinted, and it is not difficult to imagine that most of his works are never published owing to his lowly stature in the world of magical research. 

# Veil Theory
What little is known of Zikran's theories is this:

* Zikran theorises that the Veil is a magical construct eveloping Celarith which keeps things from coming and going as they please
* Zikran believes that there are not just stars and other planets, but entire planes of existence with multifaceted cultures, life forms, and societies
* Zikran believes that the fundamental essence keeping the Veil alive is some sort of magical force. He is currently conducting experiments to see if a hole can be torn in the Veil and maintained for further examination.
