Current Status: NEUTRAL

Very little is known about Archmage Vigo, other that she lives Thar-Vokar, and was the master of [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Alzathorn Ionie\|Alzathorn Ionie]]. The group have recently discovered that she runs a criminal organisation called [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]]. 

It is also known that Vigo was active during the time of the revolution in Vor'el-Thar. Vigo, afflicted with the same memory issues as others, earnestly believes that she fought on the side of the revolution against her mage kin. Precisely what happenede, or why she was made *excommunicado* by the King, though, is not clear to her. 

What little is known of Vigo is that, even without magic, is a sociopolitical *force majeur*. [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Eltura Tolsh\|Eltura Tolsh]], a woman not prone to exaggeration, noted that when Vigo realises she is being hunted, she won't be found unless she wants to be. 

The brief experience of Vigo that Penn has had so far has revealed that she is capable of very high level teleportation magic, seemingly without the need of a circle. She also managed to appear in Penn's room with ease.  She is also capable of  creating demiplanes from small precious gems for her lieutenants. More information shall surely be revealed if the party align with her and ask the right questions.
