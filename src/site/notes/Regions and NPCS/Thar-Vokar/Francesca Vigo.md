Current Status: NEUTRAL

Once an archmage and professor of metamagic at the academy in Vor'el-Thar, Vigo now runs [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]] out of a need to survive, and also to thwart the evil that has taken hold in the city. Once thought to be the master of [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Alzathorn Ionie\|Alzathorn Ionie]], she has been recovering her memories in an effort to clear name, eventually finding [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Halkith Fensterbrood\|Halkith Fensterbrood]] and capturing him for information. 

It is also known that Vigo was active during the time of the revolution in Vor'el-Thar. Vigo, afflicted with the same memory issues as others, earnestly believes that she fought on the side of the revolution against her mage kin. Precisely what happenede, or why she was made *excommunicado* by the King, though, is not clear to her. 

What little is known of Vigo is that, even without magic, is a sociopolitical *force majeur*. [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Eltura Tolsh\|Eltura Tolsh]], a woman not prone to exaggeration, noted that when Vigo realises she is being hunted, she won't be found unless she wants to be. Vigo has taken many lives, but it has never been out of choice or desire. 

The brief experience of Vigo that Penn has had so far has revealed that she is capable of very high level teleportation magic, seemingly without the need of a circle. She also managed to appear in Penn's room with ease.  She is also capable of  creating demiplanes from small precious gems for her lieutenants. More information shall surely be revealed if the party align with her and ask the right questions.  She is also an adept metamage.
