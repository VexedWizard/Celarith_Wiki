Current status (from his perspective!): ALLY

A particularly imposing figure, this half-giant man with black hair and a hawkish nose rules Thar-Vokar with an increasingly iron fist. 

Victrix was the leader of the revolution which overthrew the previous sorcerer's dynasty which tyrannically ruled the region. This revolution included The Night of A Thousand Tears, a wholesale purging of all mages great and small, innocent and guilty. Many citizens of Vor'el-Thar have a story about a friend or loved one who was innocently practicing magic being taken into a room and never returning. While the King did not instigate this event, he also did nothing to stop it, and does not regret that it happened. 

The King has become increasingly drunk on power of late, turning his ire towards [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]], with an intense desire both to "purge the heretic", and to quell the beginnings of a revolution against him which is brewing in the city as we speak.
