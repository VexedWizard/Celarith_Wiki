`'Far too fucking jovial for a bartender, if you ask me'` -[[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Dekel\|Dekel]]

Gerta is a Nyrrhemian dwarf who has long since made her home in Vor'el-Thar, and is the de facto leader of [[Factions/The Revolutionaries\|The Revolutionaries]]. 

A kindly woman with quite the adventuring past, Gerta is no slouch when it comes to combat, and anyone who's fallen foul of her is well aware of this fact. Ultimately, she fights for the city, and sees The Golden Gauntlet Inn as a microcosm of it.
