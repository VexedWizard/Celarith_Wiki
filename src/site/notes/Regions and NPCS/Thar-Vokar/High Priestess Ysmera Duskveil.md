A veiled tiefling, High Priestess Ysmera Duskveil is the High Priestess of Uncertainty at the Shifting Tides Temple in Vor'el-Thar. Although she speaks in riddles, she has served as an ultimately helpful figure to Maristela in her Shifting Tide trials. 

Diskveil revealed that she was also given Shifting Tide trials in her youth, and that it is the first step to official clergyhood in the church. She remains currently unaware that Maristela has spoken to [[Regions and NPCS/Saints/Saint Vitillia\|Saint Vitillia]].
