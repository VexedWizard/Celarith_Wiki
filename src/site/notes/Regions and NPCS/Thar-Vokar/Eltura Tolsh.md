Current status: PROFESSIONAL RELATIONSHIP

A tall, elegant lavender tiefling, Eltura Tolsh is known as one of Vor'el Thar's finest and most experienced invesitgative journalists. She is editor in chief at the Altar Gazette, a newspaper of record for the city itself and wider regions of Thar-Vokar. 

It is noted that the Gazette is more than content to reveal conspiracy involving people in all walks of life, two notable exceptions to date have been the Crown and [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]]. When questioned on this, Tolsh revealed that concerted investigation efforts into either faction would most likely end in her death at this time. While Tolsh is dogged when she needs to be, she recognises that nobody in her line of work lives long by being a hero. 

Tolsh recently hired the party to... reclaim an item of immense sentimental value to her- a small carved wooden idol she made with her brother before he was disappeared by the regime change.  In return, she promised information on The Silken Path's leadership and 5000 gold pieces.
