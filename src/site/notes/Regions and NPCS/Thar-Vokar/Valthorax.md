A scheming, conniving adolescent green dragon, Valthorax saw the chaos and opportunity of the Night of a Thousand tears and took it. Living among the people for almost ten years in his shape-shifted form, Valthorax slowly identified cracks in the armour and sanity of the city's political elite, murdering [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Corellon Flitt\|Corellon Flitt]] to install himself as majordomo to [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/King Victrix\|King Victrix]]. 

It was at that time that Valthorax began to foment misery among its peoples, as well as enrich the state for his horde. When [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]] and [[Factions/The Revolutionaries\|The Revolutionaries]] resisted, Valthorax hit upon his best plan yet- tax healing potions so heavily that they become inaccessible to most. He got his gold and his misery for a while this way, but he underestimated Vor'el-Thar's independent spirit, as well as our party of heroes. 

Valthorax had the Cowardice, Cunning and Spite keywords (see [[Pantheon and Phenomena/Known Boss Keywords\|Known Boss Keywords]] for reference).



# Qualities of the Pretender
The individual pretending to be Corellon Flitt seems to be diplomatic, but a recent interaction with Penn and Maristela revealed a much nastier streak when the characters didn't follow his guidelines for locating Francesca Vigo. Whoever this individual is, they don't like it when their control is flaunted.
# Visage of the Pretender
A tall, gaunt man with emerald eyes like his brothers [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Baron Barnabus Flitt\|Baron Barnabus Flitt]] and [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Phineas Flitt\|Phineas Flitt]], Corellon Flitt serves as the majordomo to [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/King Victrix\|King Victrix]] in Vor'el-Thar.

While initially composed, Corellon revealed how beleagured his job has rendered him to Maristela during an evening visit to the [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/The Altar Chateau\|The Altar Chateau]].  He revealed that the King has gone insane, and that [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]] is at the epicentre of a tinderbox situation that could affect the entire city. The King has put a levy on healing potions in an effort to quell a revolution, and Vigo, through [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]] is creating "black market" healing potions of the same quality, and selling them at affordable prices to the populace.  Corellon also remembers the revolution, but not what happened to Francesca Vigo during it. He reported that he cannot recall when or why he stopped remembering that specific detail of his life. 

Although Corellon has some considerable sway, he has been using most of his goodwill to prevent the king from descending into complete despotism. He has promised to assist the party wherever he can, although he is personally unsure what the resolution to this conundrum is without more information. 

Corellon, like his brothers, is charming and personable, and fundamentally willing to assist the right cause. He will, however, put nothing above his duty and those who he deems antagonistic to that duty will find themselves to have a very powerful enemy.
