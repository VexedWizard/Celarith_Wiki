A luxury hotel not far from the city's Altar Ward, the Altar Chateau serves as lodgings for Celarith's nobility, as well as a discrete meeting place for the most influential people in Vor'el-Thar. It is currently the bastion of the party, who are staying there courtesy of the Crown. 

A notable feature of the Chateau is the Concierge, who can and will provide anything requested (within the realm of reason). The party have never seen the Concierge, and have yet to avail themselves of the service directly. 

[[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]] recently revealed that she owns the hotel, and that the Concierge is one of the procurment and intelligence arms of [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]].
