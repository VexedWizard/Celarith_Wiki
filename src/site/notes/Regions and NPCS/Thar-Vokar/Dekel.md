`He's a cunt, but he's our cunt.`- [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Gerta Gundenheim\|Gerta Gundenheim]]

This cantankerous orc runs A Salt & Battery, the finest weapon shop in thar-Vokar, and quite possibly Celarith. A harsh outward demeanour hides a deeply dedicated smith who fights for his native city with every breath he takes. Now that Penn and Maristela have fought with the revolution, as well as paid full price for weapons, Dekel is their staunch ally.
