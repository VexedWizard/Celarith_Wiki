Currently in the Pearl [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]] is using to keep him prisoner, Halkith is a slimy, nasty example of a human archmage. 

Halkith was one of the mageocracy's most horrendous and relentless experimenters, focusing his research on the magical connection between twins.  Although it was initially believed that Vigo was his master, Fensterbrood confirmed to Penn and Maristela that he was in fact that master of [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Alzathorn Ionie\|Alzathorn Ionie]]. In a further shock revelation, Fensterbrood stated that he always viewd Ionie as the lesser of his apprentices, with his true prodigy being [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Sister Ivara Cael- Voice of the Emberwardens\|Sister Ivara Cael- Voice of the Emberwardens]].
