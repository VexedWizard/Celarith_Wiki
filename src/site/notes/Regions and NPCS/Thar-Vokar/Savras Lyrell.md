Current Status: CAUTIOUS ALLY

A no-nonsense wood-elf, Savras is a genius alchemist who works for the Silken Path as [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]]'s lieutenant. Fiercely loyal to her long-time friend, and equally devoted to the Path's cause. As long as the Party remain nonviolent toward the Path, Savras is willing to work with them. When they do choose a side, however, they will find themselves with a potent ally or an almighty foe.
