# Cowardice
When under X% HP, the creature will attempt to flee instead of fighting to the death. Restrain him to keep him from fleeing.

# Cunning
The creature has been studying you, and will use battle tactics which will attempt to lock down your conventional tactics. Consider using abilities not conventionally used by your characters, or terrain features to gain the advantage.

# Spite
The creature's ultimate goal is your demise, and it will prioritise downed characters in an effort to make them fail all their death saves.
