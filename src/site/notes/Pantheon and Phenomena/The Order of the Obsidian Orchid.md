An organisation seemingly comprised of powerful individuals from across the realms (including [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]] and [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Arnomar Venscribe, Archmage of the Grand Ballad\|Arnomar Venscribe, Archmage of the Grand Ballad]]), the Order of the Obsidian Orchid is an ancient and arcane sect which seeks to be prepared and present for times of great need in Celarith's history. 

Vigo, perhaps truly understanding what messes Penn and Maristela find themselves in as Chosen of Allyra and Varnir, gave them  each a [[Magic Items/Token of the Obsidian Orchid\|Token of the Obsidian Orchid]] tokens of the Obsidian Orchid which, according to her, will provide them safe passage and allies wherever they may be in the world.
