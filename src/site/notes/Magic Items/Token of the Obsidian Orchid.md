*Wondrous item, rare, requires gifting by a member of [[Pantheon and Phenomena/The Order of the Obsidian Orchid\|The Order of the Obsidian Orchid]]*

This item is able to magically morph into a ring, pendant, necklace, bracelet, or tattoo which cannot be separated from the wearer once gifted while they remain alive. The token has one charge. Once used, it disappears permanently. 

### Oh. Fuck. 
As a magic action, the wearer may speak the keyword that they set to request aid from a member of the Obsidian Orchid when they are in dire need. The token identifies the most appropriate individual depending on what kind of aid is requested, but will take requests into account. After this, the token will enact one of the following effects:
1. Permanent message: a permanent telepathic (which may be activated or disactivated at will) can be established with the relevant individual
2. Summon: if appropriate, the token will act as a *teleport* spell to the token bearer for the individual whose assistance was sought
3. Send: if appropriate, the token will act as a *teleport* spell for the token bearer to go to the  individual whose assistance was sought
