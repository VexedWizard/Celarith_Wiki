*You have gathered a great many allies on your journeys, and many of them want to hear all about your travels. A rookery is by far the most efficient way to speak to whoever you want, whenever you want.*

# Mundane Communication
**Air Mail**: ravens and crows inhabiting this rookery are able to transport letters and small packages at a pace of 30 miles a day. You are also able to  send larger packages at a rate of 15 miles per day. The rookery will also take delivery of any items sent to you in this way. 

**Homing Pigeons**: The rookery is home to homing pigeons which can transport good as above. These pigeons carry special transportation foot rings which allow you to send them to anyone who you could ordinarily cast the *Sending* or *Scrying* spells on. 


# Magical Communication
**Express Delivery**: When spending a day in the rookery, you have up to three free castings of the *Sending* spell per long rest.
