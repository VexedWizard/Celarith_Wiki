*Your knowledge of Celarith's flora, and their accompanying alchemical secrets, has allowed you to create healing potions with great efficiency.*

## Alchemical Ingenuity
You may craft any potion, elixir, or poison in the DMG i up to *very rare*  without any check required. Your greenhouse provides the base herbs to do so, however some potions require specialist ingredients (such as from specific creatures) which incur further gold cost. When crafting an item, it is expected that you spend your bastion action each week to do so.  The time and gold costs for these items are as follows:


| **Item Rarity** | **Gold Cost** | **Time Spent**    |
| --------------- | ------------- | ----------------- |
| *Common*        | 5             | 1 day             |
| *Uncommon*      | 25            | 1 bastion action  |
| *Rare*          | 250           | 2 bastion actions |
| *Very Rare*     | 2,500         | 4 bastion actions |

## Regional Roots
Cealrith's continents are full of diverse plants, some of which are entirely unique to certain regions.  Your ability to cultivate and work with these precious flora are the result of your careful appreciation for each region's local beauty, as well as the kindness shown to local alchemists who help you unveil the plants' true potential. You have the following plants and their potions growing in the greenhouse:


### Willow's Rest Wisteria
Willow's Rest is, for better or worse, a land steeped in arcane effects. This orchid grows as a result of that impact, and can be used to create magical versatility. When brewing a potion with the wisteria's leaves, the potion grants you one use of any spell on the wizard spell list, which you may case without a spell slot. You must be able to cast an equivalent level spell using a spell slot. You need not cast the spell immediately. 
 

| **Rarity**  | **Spell Level** | Gold cost | **Time Spent**    |
| ----------- | --------------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| *Uncommon*  | Cantrip & 1     | 25        | 1 bastion action  |
| *Rare*      | 2-3             | 250       | 2 bastion actions |
| *Very Rare* | 4-5             | 2,500     | 4 bastion actions |



### Vor'el Tharian Thornbloom
With your deepening understanding of both healing magic  and healing potions in Thar-Vokar, your work with the alchemists of The Silken Path have allowed you to combine your magic and the Thornbloom to create a more consistent healing potion. When using these potions, you roll as normal against a minimum healing threshold. The potion heals you at that minimum healing threshold or at whatever you rolled, whichever is higher. As these potions require a blend of prayer and alchemy as well as highly specialist ingredients, they take longer and cost more to produce. 


| **Healing Potion**         | **Rarity**  | **Gold Cost** | **Time Spent **   | **Minimum Healing Threshold** |
| -------------------------- | ----------- | ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Potion of Healing          | *Common*    | 10            | 2 days            | 7 HP (2d4 +2)                 |
| Greater Potion of Healing  | *Uncommon*  | 50            | 2 bastion actions | 14 HP (4d4+4)                 |
| Superior Potion of Healing | *Rare*      | 500           | 4 bastion actions | 28 HP (8d4+8)                 |
| Supreme Potion of Healing  | *Very Rare* | 5,000         | 8 bastion actions | 45 HP (10d4+20)               |
