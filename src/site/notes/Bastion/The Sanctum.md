*With your deepening theology and relationship to your god, you have found new and improved ways to sanctify items. Where before you relied on magical conversion, you are now able to enchant anything with religious magic, calling upon your deity to aid your path.*

## Celestial Imbuement 
You may craft any non-consumable item up to *very rare* from the relics table (DMG pp. 330-331) without any check required. When crafting an item , it is expected that you spend your bastion action each week to do so.  The time and gold costs for these items are as follows:


| **Item Rarity** | **Gold Cost** | **Time Spent**    |
| --------------- | ------------- | ----------------- |
| *Common*        | 0             | 1 bastion action  |
| *Uncommon*      | 100           | 2 bastion actions |
| *Rare*          | 1,000         | 4 bastion actions |
| *Very Rare*     | 10,000        | 8 bastion actions |

## Sigils of Parable
With every journey, the devout influence - and are influenced by - the people and places around them, religious or otherwise. As you have travelled, the people you have assisted have inspired you to create sigils which can be engraved on the items you create, and each item can only have one sigil. The costs are as follows:


| An item you are crafting          | No Cost                  |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| An existing magical/ mundane item | 50 GP + 1 bastion action |


### Sigil of the Nightingales
*Weapons only*
**Arcane Versimiltude**: You may add one extra weapon mastery to this weapon. You are considered proficient with this weapon mastery even if you do not have the Weapon Mastery feature. 

### Sigil of the Silken Path
*Armour only*
**Underground Operation**: You have advantage on stealth checks when wearing this armour. If this armour normally gives you disadvantage on stealth checks, you roll without disadvantage.
