A criminal organisation, the precise goals of which are unknown. 

Recently, the Silken Path have been dealing in healing herbs, of all things, in an effort to provide the citizens of Vor'el-Thar with an affordable means of healing. Historically, the activities of The Silken Path have been limited to scamming the Crown out of every last copper piece they can, and controlling the activity of violent criminal gangs within the city. Indeed, many upstart gangs have been run out of the city by The Path. The precise motivations behind this activity is a mystery. Further investigations are required to figure out to what end The Path carry out their activity...
