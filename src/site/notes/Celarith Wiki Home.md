# Last We Left Off...
The party were in full heist mode, with Penn and Maristela making the requisite purchases and plans to work out how to go about stealing a small wooden owlbear statue for [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Eltura Tolsh\|Eltura Tolsh]]. With the plan in action, we saw our heroes go in during broad daylight via a secret door round the back of the vault. The litany of traps in each room did not phase the group, with Concorde VI putting his keen eyes to good use to keep everyone safe and sound. Maristela was remarkably successful at convincing the guards that they were just part of the team, to the point that the guards inadvertently ended up helping with the heist. 

Alongside the required statue, the group couldn't resist taking several other shiny things, selling a few just after the heist. Of the items they took, the most interesting consists of three sheets of parchment from an encoded dossier found in Vidorant's desk. After the heist, while Maristela goes to look for lead, Penn headed to the temple of Varnir...
