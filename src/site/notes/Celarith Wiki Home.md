# Last We Left Off...

After succesfully proving themselves to the revolutionaries, our interpid party found themselves preparing to undertake their most dfearsome foe yet. All the while, a revolution was brewing in Vor'el-Thar. A revolution which, if left untamed, would have spelled certain death for [[Factions/The Revolutionaries\|The Revolutionaries]] and [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]] alike owing to Valthorax's presence. 

Artfully balancing the needs of these factions, you gathered intelligence and prepared for your foe. Penn spent considerable time with The Revolutionaries, convincing them to unite under his, [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Gerta Gundenheim\|Gerta Gundenheim]]'s and [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Dekel\|Dekel]]'s lead.

Maristela assisted [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Savras Lyrell\|Savras Lyrell]] in making enough healing potions for the city, as well as beginning to learn metamagic from Vigo, ensuring that she helped the city for the aftermath as well herself for what will come. 

By the time all was said and done, the scene was set. Lives would be lost, families torn asunder, but the horrors of the last revolution were unlikley to rear their ugly heads with such ferocity. You did battle with the Green Wyrm, surviving fear, poison, falling gold piles, and turned Valthorax's lair against him. While one little goblin may have had the killing blow, he would never have survived without you working to support each other and vanquish your foes. 

[[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Valthorax\|Valthorax]]'s reign of misery has ended.
