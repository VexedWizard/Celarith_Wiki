# Last We Left Off...
Last session's events saw a concerning and urgent political sitaution be revealed to the party by [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Corellon Flitt\|Corellon Flitt]], during a visit to [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/The Altar Chateau\|The Altar Chateau]]:  

1. [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/King Victrix\|King Victrix]] has gone insane and is obsessed with capturing [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]]
2. The King has put a levy on all healing in the city, including healing potions
3. Francesca Vigo's organisation [[Factions/The Silken Path\|The Silken Path]] has been providing "black market" healing potions to the masses as a countermeasure. 
4. Vigo was active during Vicrtix's revolution against the sorcerous dynasty which was in power, although her precise involvement is unknown to Flitt. 
5. Flitt does not know why he cannot remember anything of Vigo's involvement in the revolution

After this bombshell, the party answered the call of [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Eltura Tolsh\|Eltura Tolsh]], Vor'el-Thar's preeminent investigative journalist. Owing to a dispute with an antique collector and fellow journalist, Tolsh had an item of immense sentimental item stolen from her. If the party acquire it back, she is willing to give them 5000 gold pieces and information on The Silken Path's Leadership. 

After planning the heist, the party found themselves awaiting the next day for go time...
