# Last We Left Off...

Our (no longer so humble) adventuring party found themselves back on considerably more comfortable home turf, but not before a long journey home bore a new member of the party to the table in the form of [[Regions and NPCS/Billy\|Billy]] the druid, a particularly shy human who has a hankering for discovering all the world's plants.  After being given a small [[Magic Items/Token of the Obsidian Orchid\|token]] from Vigo each, a symbol of what is known as [[Pantheon and Phenomena/The Order of the Obsidian Orchid\|The Order of the Obsidian Orchid]], the party was able to resume their shipfaring ways back to Valethwyn. 

About a day out of arrival, a familiar voice popped into Maristela's head, that of [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Arnomar Venscribe, Archmage of the Grand Ballad\|Arnomar Venscribe, Archmage of the Grand Ballad]]. He directed our group to pay a visit to [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/King Valo IX\|King Valo IX]]'s office. There, they found the King who took them to a peculiar book- one containing the spirit of a creature called [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Gazre-Azram\|Gazre-Azram]] (**known above table to be a djinn but NOT in character**). After some odd negotiating with an entitiy that clearly does not knwo this world's ways, Gazre-Azram revealed that he was trapped in the book by a mage known colloquially as [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Zikran the Particularly Dense\|Zikran the Particularly Dense]], a mage working on Veil Theory. Gazre-Azram also reported that [[Regions and NPCS/Valedorn/Sister Ivara Cael- Voice of the Emberwardens\|Sister Ivara Cael- Voice of the Emberwardens]] had quizzed him on what he knew of Zikran's work and where to find him. Once she established that the entity knew precious little, she placed him in the restricted section at Valethwyn's main library instead of putting him back in Zikran's lair. Gazre-Azram agreed a simple proposition with the party: free him by persuading Zikran (perhaps to the death) to release him, and he will grant the party the use of one *wish* spell.

The party headed to Zikran's island lab (via the new Creekvale to drop off Wolfie and catch sight of [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/King Victrix\|King Victrix]]) where they came across an entity known as Ashgarlyth (**known above table to be an Aboleth, but NOT in character**) who showed Maristela visions of worlds, galaxies, planes, and universes beyond. Visions of creatures and entities far more ancient that she ever knew could exist. After a failed negotiation, battle was done with this creature where several of the party nearly drowned. However, a victorious party found directions to Zikran's mountain lair, a research diary, and an empty dais made for some sort of arcane device...
