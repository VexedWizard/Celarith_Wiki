# Last We Left Off...
Hooooo boy guys...

Beginning our session, we found Penn exploring The Worldspark Temple of Varnir- a building seemingly far older and more more mature in construction than many of the other in Thar-Vokar. After a quick tour from a kindly priest, Penn went to The Verdant Crucible, the domain of creation within the Temple, only to find an eerily familiar face watching over some of the more advanced martial acolytes: a grinning black dragonborn by the name of [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Archpriest Eltherain Nox\|Archpriest Eltherain Nox]]. After picking his jaw up off the floor, Penn was welcomed in and Eltherain explained to him that the Order of the Pyre is welcome in his domain. Indeed, the reason that the Temple as a whole does not approve of them is mainly due to [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Archpriestess Saelis Pyraxis\|Archpriestess Saelis Pyraxis]], and he would need to speak to her more to find out why. Eltherain encouraged Penn to train with him during his downtime, with the sole condition that Eltherain's methods are strictly adhered to. 

All the while, Maristela took the party's loot that was not sold, and went to one of her favourite coves to recuperate and ponder on the nature of their journey thus far. A well earned break! 

Once the party had reunited, they met with an irate [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Not Corellon Flitt\| Corellon Flitt]] who chastised them for undertaking the heist, and for doing it while also revealing themselves to Vidorant. Flitt seemed to know that the party had taken [[Pantheon and Phenomena/Vidorant's Three Codex Pages\|Vidorant's Three Codex Pages]] from her desk, and made them copy it down so that the originals could be returned (or so Flitt said). A perplexed and ruffled party then found themselves parlaying with [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Eltura Tolsh\|Eltura Tolsh]] who passed on information in return for her statue, although seemed to show some apprehension as to what the party where going to do with it, as if she had not fully contemplated the consequences of her actions. The information revealed that a nearby town had been raided by bandits, and in deciding to fight back, the villagers had suffered major casualties as well as exempted themselves from state healing capabilities. As such, they were likely to seek the Silken Path out, if for no other reason than desperation. 

On their journey, after having all their gold stolen, the party came across the town in question, and the very apparent destruction. The party brought their healing miracles to the townsfolk, both in the town and at the heavily veiled mobile hospital run by the Path. Not only did they earn the adoration of the townsfolk and the healthy respect of the Silken Path, earning them a discussion with [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Savras Lyrell\|Savras Lyrell]] under truth serum. A meeting with [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Francesca Vigo\|Francesca Vigo]] was agreed then, and during the wee hours of the morning she appeared in Penn's suite without warning, taking him to an undisclosed location without his weapons or armour. There she revealed that whoever the party had been talking to, it was [[Regions and NPCS/Thar-Vokar/Not Corellon Flitt\|Not Corellon Flitt]]...
